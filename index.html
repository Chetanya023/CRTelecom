Good to know

Questions before you connect?

Here are a few things teams commonly want to understand before getting started.

Communications Platform as a Service (CPaaS) lets businesses add communication capabilities such as SMS, voice calls and messaging to their applications using APIs and software tools.

CPaaS provides programmable communication building blocks. CCaaS provides a cloud-based contact-center environment for agents, routing, queues, reporting and customer support workflows.

Yes. A modular rollout can begin with one channel and expand as your use cases, customer needs and integration maturity evolve.

That depends on the services you choose. APIs and webhooks suit custom application integrations; a web dashboard or managed onboarding may be more suitable for teams that prefer a lower-code setup.

Implementation should account for applicable telecom rules, sender registration and template requirements, consent, opt-outs, data protection and each channel provider’s policies. Exact requirements depend on your use case and destination.
